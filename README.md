# AI Poverty Social Good Project

## 1. The Problem and Who It Affects

Poverty in the Bay Area affects many people despite the region’s overall wealth, especially low-income families and workers in stable but modest-paying jobs. Individuals like Jennifer, who work in government or public-sector roles, often earn incomes that are not enough to keep up with the high cost of rent, food, and basic living expenses. As a result, even full-time workers can struggle to meet their needs and may face ongoing financial stress.

Our project focuses on using AI to make it easier for people facing poverty to find and access support resources. By providing personalized guidance and clear information, the system can help users quickly understand what assistance they qualify for, such as food programs or housing aid. The goal is to reduce confusion, save time, and connect people like Jennifer to the help they need more efficiently.
