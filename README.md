# AI Poverty Social Good Project

## 1. The Problem and Who It Affects

Poverty affects many people in the Bay Area, especially low-income families and people like Jennifer. Many people like Jennifer work in government or state jobs that do not pay enough to afford food and rent.

Our project focuses on using AI to help connect people facing poverty with useful support resources. The goal is to make it easier for people to find help quickly and understand what options may be available to them.
